# Student FAQ

## Fees

**When are semester fees due?**
Semester fees are due within the first fifteen days of the semester. A late fee
of Rs. 100 per day applies after that, capped at Rs. 3000. Students with unpaid
fees cannot collect their examination hall ticket.

**Can fees be paid in instalments?**
Yes. A student may request a two-instalment plan from the Accounts Office
before the due date. The second instalment must be cleared before the
end-semester examination begins.

**Is there a scholarship?**
The merit scholarship covers 50% of tuition for students with a CGPA of 9.0 or
above and no backlogs. It is applied automatically at the start of the next
semester; no application is needed.

## Library

**How many books can I borrow?**
Undergraduate students may borrow four books at a time for fourteen days.
Postgraduate students may borrow six books for twenty-one days. Reference books
and journals cannot be taken out of the library.

**What is the fine for a late return?**
Rs. 5 per book per day. Borrowing privileges are suspended once the outstanding
fine crosses Rs. 200.

**What are the library timings?**
The library is open from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM on weekdays and 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM
on Saturdays. It is closed on Sundays and public holidays. During the
examination period the library stays open until 11:00 PM.

## Hostel

**What is the hostel curfew?**
Hostel gates close at 10:00 PM. Late entry requires prior written permission
from the warden.

**Can I stay in the hostel during vacations?**
Yes, with prior approval from the warden and payment of the vacation mess
charges. Requests must be made at least one week before the vacation starts.

## Academics and records

**How do I get a bonafide certificate?**
Apply through the student portal under Services. The certificate is issued
within three working days and can be collected from the Academic Office.

**How do I change my elective subject?**
Elective changes are allowed only during the first two weeks of the semester,
subject to seat availability, and require the signature of both the current and
the new subject teacher.

**Where can I see my attendance and marks?**
Both are published on the student portal. Attendance is updated at the end of
every month and internal marks are published within ten days of each
assessment.

**Who do I contact about a timetable clash?**
Report timetable clashes to the class coordinator in the first week of the
semester. Clashes reported later cannot usually be resolved for that semester.

## Identity card

**I lost my ID card. What do I do?**
Report the loss at the Security Office and apply for a duplicate through the
student portal. The replacement fee is Rs. 200 and the new card is issued
within five working days. Entry to the examination hall without an ID card is
not permitted.
