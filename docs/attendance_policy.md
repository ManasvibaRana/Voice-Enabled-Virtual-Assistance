# Attendance Policy

## Minimum attendance requirement

Every student must maintain at least **75% attendance** in each registered
subject to be eligible to sit the end-semester examination. Attendance is
calculated per subject, not as an overall average across subjects, so a strong
record in one subject cannot compensate for a weak record in another.

Attendance is recorded by the subject teacher at the start of each scheduled
class. A student who arrives more than ten minutes after the class begins is
marked absent for that session.

## Condonation

A student whose attendance falls between 65% and 74.99% in a subject may apply
for condonation. Condonation is granted only once per semester and only for
documented reasons: hospitalisation, a death in the immediate family, or
participation in an official college event. The application must be submitted
to the Head of Department within seven days of the shortage being published,
together with supporting documents.

A condonation fee of Rs. 500 per subject applies. Attendance below 65% cannot
be condoned under any circumstances, and the student must repeat the subject in
the next available semester.

## Medical leave

Medical leave is counted as absence for attendance calculation, but a medical
certificate from a registered practitioner submitted within five days of return
makes the student eligible for condonation as described above. Certificates
submitted late are not considered.

## Official college events

Students representing the college in sports, cultural festivals, hackathons or
academic conferences are marked present for the days covered by the official
duty letter issued by the Dean of Student Affairs. The duty letter must be
issued before the event, not after it.

## Publication and correction of records

Attendance is published on the student portal at the end of every month.
Students have ten days from publication to raise a correction request with the
subject teacher. After ten days the record is treated as final and no further
correction is possible.

Detention for shortage of attendance is announced two weeks before the
end-semester examination begins, so that affected students have time to appeal.
