# Examination and Grading Policy

## Components of assessment

Each subject is assessed out of 100 marks in total, split as follows:

- **Quiz** - 20 marks, best two of three quizzes are counted.
- **Midterm** - 50 marks, scaled down to 30 marks in the final total.
- **End-semester (final) examination** - 100 marks, scaled down to 50 marks.

A student must score at least 40% in the end-semester examination separately,
in addition to 40% in the total, to pass a subject.

## Grade bands

Grades are awarded on the total of 100 marks:

| Marks     | Grade | Grade points |
|-----------|-------|--------------|
| 90 to 100 | O     | 10           |
| 80 to 89  | A     | 9            |
| 70 to 79  | B     | 8            |
| 60 to 69  | C     | 7            |
| 50 to 59  | D     | 6            |
| 40 to 49  | E     | 5            |
| below 40  | F     | 0            |

A grade of F means the subject must be repeated. There is no rounding of marks
at grade boundaries; 79.9 is a B, not an A.

## SGPA and CGPA

SGPA is the credit-weighted average of grade points earned in a semester. CGPA
is the credit-weighted average across all semesters completed so far. Subjects
with an F grade are included in the calculation with zero grade points until
the subject is cleared, at which point the new grade replaces the F.

## Re-evaluation

A student may apply for re-evaluation of an end-semester answer script within
fifteen days of the result being published. The fee is Rs. 800 per subject and
is refunded in full if the revised marks differ by five or more marks. Quizzes
and midterm papers are not eligible for re-evaluation; they are only eligible
for re-totalling, which is free and must be requested within five days.

Re-evaluation results are final. A second re-evaluation of the same script is
not permitted, and marks may go down as well as up.

## Supplementary examinations

Supplementary examinations for failed subjects are held once, six weeks after
the regular results are declared. A student may appear for a maximum of three
supplementary subjects in one session. The maximum grade obtainable in a
supplementary examination is B.

## Malpractice

Any case of malpractice reported during an examination is referred to the
Examination Committee. Confirmed malpractice results in the cancellation of all
papers of that semester, and a repeat offence results in debarment for one full
academic year.
